Policy & FAQs

Loan Policy

📜 Loan Policy & Terms
How is the Personal (Starter) loan interest calculated?

A flat % of the principal is charged per month. Example: MWK 100,000 for 1 month = MWK 125,000 repayable.

How is the Business (Nthandizi) loan calculated?

A flat % of the principal for the full -month period, split into equal monthly installments. Example: MWK 300,000 = MWK 450,000 repayable (MWK 150,000 / month).

What is Chikole?

Chikole is collateral (property such as land, a house, a vehicle or household items) pledged to secure a loan.
